# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

ALX Data learners use the public hub to find credible portfolio work that matches their course, skill level, and career interests. Technical Mentors and authorised ALX staff use a private authoring area to publish and maintain project briefs without editing code.

## Product Purpose

Turn a static collection of project pages into a focused, searchable project library. Success means learners can quickly choose a worthwhile project and understand what strong work requires, while staff can keep the library current without developer assistance.

## Brand Personality

Credible, focused, and encouraging. The experience should feel professionally demanding without becoming academic, corporate, or intimidating.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS marketing template, a gamified learning platform, or a dense LMS dashboard. Avoid ornamental cards, inflated feature claims, decorative gradients, and interface elements that do not help a learner choose or complete a project.

## Design Principles

1. Put project substance before promotion.
2. Make scanning easy and reading comfortable.
3. Use ALX colour as a navigational signal, not decoration.
4. Keep learner choices clear and staff workflows familiar.
5. Prefer flexible content and simple systems over speculative features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, clear language, responsive layouts, and readable long-form project briefs across common laptop and mobile screens.
